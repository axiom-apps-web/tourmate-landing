Frequently asked questions

Questions? We're happy to answer them.

What happens to my tourists' data?

It's encrypted and used only to keep your tours safe, we never sell it. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Do you help us implement it?

Yes. Every agency plan includes onboarding, guide training, and initial hardware setup.

Does it work without internet signal?

Yes. TourMate is built for the backcountry: it stores data locally and syncs automatically the moment a signal is available.

What if a tourist has a real medical emergency?

TourMate is a monitoring and alert tool, not a replacement for emergency services, but it gives your guide the information they need to respond fast.
